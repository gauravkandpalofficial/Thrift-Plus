\# Thrift+ — Project Requirements



\## 1. Project Overview



\*\*Project Name:\*\* Thrift+



\*\*Project Type:\*\* Web-based clothing marketplace



\*\*Primary Goal:\*\* Build a responsive marketplace for affordable pre-owned clothing and eligible low-cost clothing products in India.



\## 2. Problem Statement



Local clothing markets offer affordable products, but buyers may have difficulty discovering products online. Sellers may also have limited digital reach and inconsistent pricing.



Thrift+ aims to provide an online platform where buyers can discover clothing and sellers can list eligible products under clearly defined rules.



\## 3. Core Features



\- Responsive homepage and product browsing.

\- Product search and category filters.

\- Seller accounts and product listings.

\- Human-led pricing review for eligible products.

\- Approved price ranges for reviewed products.

\- Buyer orders and inventory management.

\- Seller subscription plans and monthly review credits.

\- Shipping status and basic order tracking.

\- Product reporting and trust-and-safety controls.



\## 4. Pricing Rules



\- Products priced below ₹150 may follow the direct-listing path after basic checks.

\- Products priced at ₹150 or above require human review.

\- A reviewer records the condition grade and approved minimum and maximum price.

\- The seller must select a price within the approved range before publishing a reviewed product.

\- The backend must enforce these rules.



\## 5. Seller Plans



\- \*\*Free Seller:\*\* Two human-reviewed product submissions per month.

\- \*\*Regular Seller:\*\* ₹29 per month for fifteen human-reviewed product submissions.

\- Eligible products below ₹150 do not consume review credits.

\- Requests for additional information about an existing review do not consume another credit.



\## 6. User Roles



\- Guest

\- Buyer

\- Seller

\- Reviewer

\- Administrator



Each role must have appropriate permissions.



\## 7. Initial Technology Stack



\- Frontend: React and Vite

\- Styling: CSS

\- Backend: Node.js and Express

\- Database: PostgreSQL

\- Version control: Git and GitHub



\## 8. Development Approach



Build the project in small milestones. Test each feature before moving to the next milestone.



Initially, use sample product data and simulated checkout and shipping. Introduce backend services, database storage and external integrations incrementally.



\## 9. Initial Success Criteria



\- The homepage works on desktop and mobile.

\- Product cards display sample clothing data.

\- Search and category filters work.

\- Product details can be opened.

\- The code is organized and documented.

\- The developer can explain the implemented features and their limitations.



\## 10. Project Status



\*\*Current milestone:\*\* Repository setup and requirements documentation.



\*\*Next milestone:\*\* React frontend foundation.
